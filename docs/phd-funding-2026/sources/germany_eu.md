# GERMANY + EU-WIDE — PhD funding for a German citizen
Retrieval date for every entry: **2026-09-07**. Every URL below was opened with WebFetch unless explicitly marked "could not open".

## Candidate facts that drive the analysis
- German citizen, age ~42 (born ~1983/84).
- M.Sc. Marine Geosciences, Univ. Bremen, defended **23 March 2026** → "last degree" clocks start March 2026. This is the single most important eligibility asset.
- Earlier M.Sc. Bochum 2011; doctoral **enrolment** at RUB 2016–2018, abandoned, **no doctorate awarded**.
- German C1 (TestDaF), English C1 (UNIcert III). No IELTS/TOEFL.

**Headline structural finding for this region:** German *Begabtenförderungswerke* count the clock from the **last qualifying degree** (his is March 2026 — excellent) and/or from **how far the dissertation has progressed** (he is at zero — excellent). Not one of the ten foundations checked has a clause excluding an applicant for a *previously abandoned* doctoral enrolment, and none asks how long ago you were *first* enrolled in a doctorate. The 2016–18 Bochum episode is therefore **not a formal bar anywhere I could verify** — but see §9 and the per-programme `notes`, because two works (HSS, RLS) count *previously received public funding* against the maximum, and Studienstiftung says it funds "in der Regel nur die erste Promotion". If the 2016–18 period was unfunded and produced no degree, both are harmless; **this must be disclosed truthfully in the CV, because all of them require a "stringenter und schlüssiger Lebenslauf" / complete education history.**

Prior Studienstiftung funding is not required. This is the single strongest German stipend fit for him — the 4-year-3-month clock is the binding constraint everywhere and his March 2026 M.Sc. resets it.

# C. How funding works in this region

There are two completely different financial models and they are not interchangeable. **(1) Stipendium** (all ten Begabtenförderungswerke, DBU, DAAD): a tax-free grant with no employment relationship — the BMFTR states "Die Promotionsstipendien betragen derzeit 1.550 Euro pro Monat. Ab dem Wintersemester 2025/2026 erhalten Stipendiatinnen und Stipendiaten monatlich 1.650 Euro. Zusätzlich zu den Stipendiengeldern erhalten Promovierende eine monatliche Forschungskostenpauschale von 100 Euro" (https://www.bmftr.bund.de/DE/Forschung/Wissenschaftssystem/WissenschaftlicheKarriere/Promotionsstipendium/promotionsstipendium.html, opened 2026-09-07). So the **current joint BMFTR/BMBF standard rate is EUR 1,650 + EUR 100 = EUR 1,750 per month**, confirmed independently on Studienstiftung (1.650 + max. 100 = "bis zu 1.750 Euro"), KAS ("höchstens 1.650 Euro im Monat" + 100 €), HSS ("monatlich 1.650 € zuzüglich einer Forschungskostenpauschale von 100 €"), FES ("monatlich 1650 Euro, sowie eine monatliche Forschungskostenpauschale von 100 Euro"), Böll ("ab 10/2025 1650,00 € Grundstipendium/Monat" + 100 €), Avicenna and DBU ("Die Höhe des Stipendiums beträgt monatlich 1.650,- €", + 150 € Sachmittel). Cusanuswerk's funding page still shows the old 1,550 + 100 while its application page shows 1,750 — the works update at different speeds. A stipend is **not taxed as employment income, but it also buys no pension entitlement, no unemployment insurance and no employer health-insurance contribution** (most works refund only 50 % of health-insurance costs up to €100/month), and side earnings are capped (Studienstiftung/KAS: €3,070/year allowance; KAS bars paid work over 20 h/month). German public universities charge **no tuition**, only a semester contribution of roughly €300–400, so a stipend of €1,750 genuinely covers a PhD — it is "fully funded" in the sense the brief requires, with the caveat that social security is on him. **(2) Employment** (MSCA, ERC teams, DFG Graduiertenkollegs, AWI/GEOMAR/Hereon/MPG posts): a TV-L E13 contract, usually at 65–75 %, with full social security, pension accrual and paid leave — the DFG describes GRK funding as "Posts for doctoral researchers and postdocs" and the MSCA Work Programme makes employment mandatory ("must recruit each eligible doctoral candidate under an employment contract or equivalent direct contract ... with full social security coverage"). Net monthly take-home on a 65 % E13 is broadly comparable to a €1,750 stipend, but the employment route is materially better long-term because of pension and unemployment cover, and it requires no proposal-writing or values-alignment.

| Pattern | Works using it | Effect on this candidate |
|---|---|---|
| Time since **last qualifying degree** | Studienstiftung ("nicht länger als vier Jahre und drei Monate"); KAS ("nicht länger als fünf Jahre ... monatsweise Rechnung") | **Passes easily** — M.Sc. March 2026 |
| Time already spent **on the current dissertation project** | Villigst ("noch nicht länger als ein Jahr an dem Projekt gearbeitet", Stichtag = submission of the application); Cusanuswerk (early phase); Avicenna ("in der Anfangszeit") | **Passes** if he applies at or near the start of the new doctorate |
| No clock, only a **negative** "not in the closing phase" test | FES ("bis circa zwei Jahre vor Abgabe"); Böll ("Abschlussförderung ... Nicht möglich"); FNS ("Dissertationen in der Endphase" excluded); KAS ("Promotionen in der Schlussphase"); RLS; Hans-Böckler ("die Promotion so weit fortgeschritten ist, dass sie innerhalb eines Jahres abgeschlossen werden kann") | **Passes** — he is at zero |

**Two works do count something backwards, and both count *money*, not enrolment:** HSS ("aus öffentlichen Mitteln bereits bis zur Höchstförderungsdauer (= 3 Jahre) unterstützt worden sind ... wird ... auf die Gesamtförderungszeit angerechnet") and RLS (prior funded doctoral time counts against its three-year limit). If the 2016–18 Bochum enrolment carried no public stipend or TV-L post, neither clause reduces anything.

**The one genuine residual risk is not a funder rule at all** — it is the university's own Promotionsordnung, which governs re-admission to a doctorate in the same subject after an abandoned attempt, and the WissZeitVG fixed-term quota if he held a paid academic post in 2016–18. Both must be checked with the target faculty before any application is submitted; neither could be resolved from any funder page.
